About Verdant

A garden that grows while you sleep — and forgives you on Mondays.

What is this?

Verdant is a cosy botanical game and a gentle plant-care companion. Every pot holds a real species with real care needs. Plants grow in real time, follow the seasons of your hemisphere, react to (simulated) weather and the actual time of day, and keep growing while the tab is closed.

Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no trackers, no servers — just paper, paint and a little bit of localStorage.

How to play

  • Tap an empty pot to plant. Match the plant's light needs to the spot.
  • Pick the watering can and press and hold over a plant. Stop before the saucer overflows.
  • Use the shears on yellowing leaves. Snip, snip.
  • Feed in spring and summer. Plants rest in winter (as should you).
  • Keyboard: H W P M F pick tools, Enter uses a tool on the focused pot.
